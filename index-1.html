About Us

A friendly place to build real English skills

Shofiul English Academy Center is an educational center dedicated to providing practical and accessible English education for students and learners.

Our goal is to create a friendly learning environment where students can develop strong English skills through regular lessons, speaking practice, activities, and guidance.

Our Mission Why we teach

To help learners speak English confidently and communicate effectively in their education, career, and everyday life.

Our Vision Where we're headed

To become a trusted English-learning center that inspires students to learn, practice, and achieve their goals.

Our Courses

Six tracks, one goal: confident communication

Pick a starting point — every course is built around simple explanations, real examples, and plenty of practice.

Spoken English

Improve your everyday English speaking and communication skills through practical conversation and speaking activities.

English Grammar

Learn English grammar from basic to advanced levels with simple explanations, structures, examples, and exercises.

Vocabulary Building

Expand your vocabulary and learn how to use new words naturally in sentences and conversations.

Pronunciation & Accent

Develop clearer pronunciation, improve speaking habits, and practice English sounds.

Reading & Writing

Improve reading comprehension, sentence formation, paragraph writing, and general English writing skills.

English for Students

Build a strong foundation in English for school, college, examinations, and future studies.
